Who issues it, who manages it, where it trades

In two sentences

Every instrument on Framont Access sits inside a chain of separately regulated parties: an issuer that owes the investor repayment, a venue where the instrument trades, a portfolio manager that takes the investment decisions, and post-trade agents that settle and pay. Portfolio management for every referenced instrument is performed by Framont & Partners Management Ltd, an AIFM authorised by the Malta Financial Services Authority; strategy developers supply the underlying strategy only and do not hold investor money.

Every instrument on this platform sits inside a regulated chain — issuer, venue, portfolio manager, post-trade agents. Each chain is drawn out one stop at a time, with the licences behind the platform set out at the end.

Why the chain matters

Reading a product summary tells you what a strategy intends to do. Reading its chain tells you who you are actually exposed to if something fails. The two are different questions, and only the second is answered by naming the parties. An instrument can have an excellent strategy and an issuer you would not lend to; the chain is where that shows up.

The licences behind the platform

  • Malta Financial Services Authority. Framont & Partners Management Ltd is licensed by the MFSA under the Investment Services Act, 1994 (Chapter 370 of the Laws of Malta). A copy of the licence is available on the MFSA website and on request.
  • AIFMD and UCITS. The company qualifies as a UCITS Management Company under the UCITS Directive and as an Alternative Investment Fund Manager under the AIFMD, the framework under which every fund in the range is managed.
  • PRIIPs and MiFID II. Listed instruments carry a Key Information Document under the PRIIPs Regulation; distribution and suitability follow MiFID II. Read the KID and the prospectus or final terms before investing.
  • Roles, kept separate. Strategy developers provide the underlying strategy only. Portfolio management of every referenced instrument is performed by Framont & Partners Management Ltd.
